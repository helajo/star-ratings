How each category is scored各类别评分说明

  • MACD: zero-axis position of a fresh golden/death cross, trend context (price vs. 200-day average), and a simple two-half divergence check between price and the MACD line.
  • RSI: read in the context of trend (price above/below its 200-day average) rather than fixed 70/30 levels, per the handbook's own critique of the naive rule.
  • Bollinger Bands: a bandwidth squeeze (lowest 20th percentile of the past ~6 months), and band touches read against the trend context.
  • Volume/OBV: the price-vs-volume quadrant (confirmed vs. unconfirmed moves), extreme volume days, and OBV crossing its own 20-day average.
  • MACD：看新出现的金叉/死叉相对零轴的位置、趋势背景（价格对比200日均线），以及价格与MACD线之间简单的两段式背离检查。
  • RSI：结合趋势（价格在200日均线之上/之下）解读，而非套用固定的70/30水平，这也是手册对朴素规则的批评。
  • 布林带：带宽收窄（处于过去约6个月最低的20%分位），以及结合趋势背景解读触及上下轨。
  • 成交量/OBV：价量象限（放量确认 vs 缺乏确认）、极端成交量日，以及OBV上穿/下穿自身20日均线。
